Flex Legends: options

A deck where one Legend slot changes between lists, like YYR with Viktor or Muamar or a third pick. Each slot has a main Legend and any number of alternates, ordered by how often they're played. Stand-in art throughout. Pick one option per row, or mix them.

The flexible strip has card edges peeking above it, as if more cards are stacked behind, plus a +2 count in the same yellow-on-black as copy counts. Reads as "more options here" without showing faces.

T3 Color pips

YYR

Small dots at the bottom of the strip, one per alternate, in that Legend's color. The quietest option; it says "this flexes" but not to what.

3 · On the deck page

D1 Alternates as named chips

Under the main Legend's name, a chip for each alternate: a small face and first name. Easy to read on a slide, and scales to 3 or 4 alternates.

D2 Mini cards under the Legend

Small full cards in a row under the flexible Legend. More visual and less readable; works best with 1 or 2 alternates.

Alternates fan out behind the main Legend, dimmed, with a +2 count. Matches T2 on the tiles; shows the alternates' colors but not their names.

My pick

  • E1 for setting it: one + under the Legend, no extra screen. The share % from E2 can come later if you want it.
  • T1 split strip on tiles: shows the actual alternates, scales to several, and needs no label.
  • D1 named chips on the deck page: presenters will say the names out loud, so the slide should show them.
  • F1 flex group on the full decklist: same idea as D1, in card form.
